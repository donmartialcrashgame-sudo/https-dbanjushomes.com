How to Sign In to DBH

Learn how to securely sign in to your DBH account, recover access, use the account area and troubleshoot common sign-in problems.

Step-by-step guide

Use the instructions below to understand this DBH page and complete the relevant task safely.

01

Open the DBH sign-in page

Use the official DBH website and open the Sign In page. Check that the address begins with https://dbanjushomes.com before entering your account information.

02

Enter your email address

Enter the email address connected to your DBH account. Check spelling carefully because the address identifies the account you are trying to access.

03

Enter your password

Type your current DBH password. Keep your password private and never send it to another person, including someone claiming to provide support.

04

Review Remember me

On a trusted personal device you may use Remember me. On a shared or public computer, leave it disabled and sign out when finished.

05

Select Sign in

Submit the form once and wait for DBH to authenticate the account. Avoid repeatedly clicking the button while the request is processing.

06

Use Forgot password when necessary

If you cannot remember your password, use the Forgot password link instead of creating another account.

07

Social sign-in availability

Google, Facebook, X, GitHub and GitLab are displayed on the DBH authentication pages but are currently disabled until their provider configuration is completed.

08

After successful sign-in

Use the authenticated DBH sidebar to access your dashboard, saved properties, property requests, alerts, profile and security settings.
