How funding works
1. Generate your dedicated bank account in the section below.
2. Transfer money from your banking app.
3. Your payment is confirmed securely.
4. Your wallet balance and transaction history update.
Transaction alerts
Device notifications are managed by DBH on this device. Once enabled, transaction alerts can arrive even when the page is closed.
If a payment succeeds but the balance does not update, refresh the wallet after a short wait and contact DBH support with the payment reference.
