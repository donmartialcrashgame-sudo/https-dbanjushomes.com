How to Use the DBH User Agreement

A public guide for understanding the DBH User Agreement before creating an account, listing property or using marketplace features.

Step-by-step guide

Use the instructions below to understand this DBH page and complete the relevant task safely.

01

Read before registering

Review the agreement before creating an account so you understand the basic rules for using DBH.

02

Understand account responsibilities

Review your responsibility to provide accurate information and protect your credentials.

03

Review property information

Understand that property descriptions, prices, availability and other listing information can change and important details should be verified.

04

Review acceptable use

Check the rules before submitting content, making requests or using marketplace features.

05

Understand property submissions

If you submit a listing or other content, make sure you have the right to provide it and that the information is not knowingly misleading.

06

Review third-party services

Authentication, hosting, storage, maps, communications and notifications may involve external providers with their own terms.

07

Understand account restrictions

Review when access may be restricted to protect users, the platform or legal obligations.

08

Know where to ask questions

If you are unsure about platform use, contact DBH through the published contact channel.
