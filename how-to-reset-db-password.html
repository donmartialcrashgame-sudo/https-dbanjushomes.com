How to Reset Your DBH Password

Step-by-step public instructions for recovering a forgotten DBH password and setting a new secure password.

Step-by-step guide

Use the instructions below to understand this DBH page and complete the relevant task safely.

01

Open Forgot password

Start from the DBH Sign In page and select Forgot password.

02

Enter your account email

Enter the email address associated with your DBH account and submit the recovery request.

03

Check your inbox

Look for the DBH password recovery message. Also check spam or junk folders if it does not appear in the main inbox.

04

Open the secure recovery link

Use the link from the recovery message. Do not forward recovery links to other people.

05

Enter a new password

Choose a new password with at least eight characters. A unique passphrase is preferable to a short or reused password.

06

Confirm the password

Enter the same new password in the confirmation field.

07

Update the password

Select Update password and wait for the authentication service to complete the change.

08

Sign in again

Return to the DBH Sign In page and use the new password. If the recovery link has expired, request a fresh one.
