HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

How to recover a forgotten DBH password

Use this page when you know your DBH account email but cannot remember the password. The recovery process sends a secure reset link instead of asking you to tell anyone your old password.

01 — Enter your account email

Type the email address associated with your DBH account. Use the same address you normally use to sign in.

02 — Send the reset request

Select Send reset link once. DBH will submit the recovery request to the authentication service.

03 — Check your inbox

Look for the password recovery email. If it is not visible, check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the correct email address.

04 — Open the secure link

Use the link from the recovery email. It takes you to the DBH password reset page where you can choose a new password.

05 — Create a new password

Choose a new password of at least eight characters. Avoid reusing a password that protects another important service.

06 — Return to sign in

After the password is updated, return to the DBH sign-in page and use the new password.

If the email does not arrive

  • Check the spelling of the email address.
  • Check spam, junk and promotional folders.
  • Wait a short time before requesting another link.
  • Use the newest recovery email if you requested more than one.
  • Contact DBH through the published support contact if you continue to have difficulty.

Security reminder

DBH will not need you to send your password by email. Keep recovery links private and do not forward them to other people.
