Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

DBH — D Banjus Homes Nig Ltd (“DBH”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use our website and property services.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when creating an account, saving a property, making a request, listing a property or contacting us, including your name, email address and details you choose to provide.

2. Authentication

We use authentication services to secure accounts. When you use Google, Facebook, X, GitHub or GitLab sign-in, the provider may share information permitted by its authorization process.

3. How information is used

  • Provide and operate DBH services.
  • Authenticate and protect accounts.
  • Respond to enquiries and property requests.
  • Maintain, improve and secure the website.
  • Send service-related messages and alerts where appropriate.

4. Storage and service providers

DBH may use hosting, database, email, notification, authentication and other technical providers to operate the service. Information may be processed by those providers as necessary to provide their services.

5. Cookies and local storage

We may use essential cookies, local storage and similar technologies for sessions, preferences and security. Optional analytics or notification technologies may be used when enabled.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may contact DBH about account information, corrections or privacy questions. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal or operational reasons.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: info@dbanjushomes.com.
