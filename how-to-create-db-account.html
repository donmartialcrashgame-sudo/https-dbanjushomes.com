How to Create a DBH Account

A complete public guide to creating a DBH property marketplace account and preparing it for secure use.

Step-by-step guide

Use the instructions below to understand this DBH page and complete the relevant task safely.

01

Open Create Account

Open the official DBH registration page from the website navigation. You can also reach it from the Sign In page.

02

Enter your full name

Provide your name accurately. This information helps associate your account with future property enquiries and account activity.

03

Enter an accessible email

Use an email address that you control and can access. It can be needed for authentication and password recovery.

04

Create a strong password

Use at least eight characters and choose a password that is difficult to guess. Do not reuse an important password from another service.

05

Confirm the password

Enter the same password again. The two password values must match before registration can continue.

06

Read the legal documents

Review the DBH User Agreement and Privacy Policy before accepting them. These documents explain platform rules and information handling.

07

Create the account

Select Create account once and wait for the result. Refreshing repeatedly can make troubleshooting harder.

08

Start using DBH

After authentication, your account can use available property-seeker features such as saved properties, requests, alerts and profile tools.
