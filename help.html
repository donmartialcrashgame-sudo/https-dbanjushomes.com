How to use DBH

DBH is designed to take you from property discovery to your next conversation in a few clear steps. You can browse publicly, while account features such as saved properties require sign-in.

01

Open the DBH marketplace

Start at the home page or go directly to Properties. Use the main search or the marketplace filters to narrow what you are looking for. You can also jump directly to Land for Sale or Commercial Property.

HomePropertiesLandCommercial
02

Search by location, property type or property ID

Use the property marketplace to search by a location such as Lagos, Ogun, Lekki or Ikorodu, choose a property type, and enter a minimum or maximum price. You can also search using a DBH property code when one is available.

03

Open a property listing

Select a property card to view the full listing. Review the title, price, location, provider information, property code, gallery and available verification details before making an enquiry.

04

Save properties you want to compare later

Use the heart button on a property card or property page to save a listing. Saved properties are kept per DBH account and browser on your device, so you can return to Saved properties later.

05

Send an enquiry

From a listing, choose the enquiry/contact option. Include your name, email, phone number and what you want to know. Property-specific enquiries can identify the listing automatically so DBH can understand the context.

Contact DBHProperty enquiry
06

Use Customer Care

The headset icon appears throughout the site. Open it to message DBH Customer Care. Signed-in customers can send messages through the DBH support system; visitors can use the provided contact route.

PROPERTY SEARCH

Finding and understanding properties

Use the marketplace as your discovery layer. Always review the complete listing and verification information before taking important property decisions.

A

Search by location

Enter a state, city, area or other relevant location in the search/filter controls. The site may use location information only when you explicitly allow the relevant browser permission.

B

Filter by property type

Choose the category that matches your need. DBH currently groups opportunities around homes/properties, land and commercial property.

C

Compare prices

Use minimum and maximum price filters or sort results from low to high or high to low. Currency options are available in the DBH interface where supported.

D

Use the property code

A listing may have a DBH property code such as DBH-XXXXXXXXXX. Keep the code in your enquiry when asking about a specific listing.

E

Review the provider

Check who is presenting the listing, including any provider verification information shown on the page. Verification status can help you interpret what has been reviewed.

F

Open the full listing

Use the property page rather than relying only on the card. The full page is where additional details, gallery content, verification information and enquiry options can appear.

TRUST & REVIEW

Understanding verification

Verification status is information about the review state of a listing. It should not be treated as a substitute for independent legal, title or professional due diligence.

A listing marked Verified Property or showing verification information has a review state recorded by DBH. A listing marked Verification pending has not reached that state. Demo listings and test records are not evidence of real-world title or ownership.
V

Verified

Use the listing's displayed verification information to understand that DBH has recorded it as verified.

P

Pending

Pending means the listing has not reached the displayed verified state. Treat it accordingly when evaluating the opportunity.

D

Demo / test records

Demo properties or documentation used for development are clearly described and should never be interpreted as proof of legal ownership.

YOUR DBH ACCOUNT

Sign-in, profile and dashboard

You can browse public pages without an account. Sign in when you want account-specific tools such as saved properties and dashboard features.

1

Google sign-in

DBH uses Google Identity Services with the DBH authentication server for Google sign-in. Supabase Auth is not used as the Google login provider.

2

Email and password

Where enabled, use your DBH email/password flow. Password recovery is available from the Forgot password page.

3

Profile

Use My profile to view and manage account information available in the DBH interface.

4

Dashboard

The dashboard brings account tools together. Some sections require sign-in and may depend on your account status.

5

Notifications

When notification permission is enabled, DBH can show account and property-related notifications on supported devices.

6

Sign out

Use the account controls to sign out. On shared computers, always sign out when finished.

PROPERTY OWNERS & AGENTS

How to list a property

DBH can accept property submissions for review before publication.

01

Prepare accurate information

Have the location, property type, asking price and descriptive information ready. Accurate information makes review and publication easier.

02

Open List a Property

Start from List a property. Follow the form and provide the information requested by DBH.

03

Provide supporting information where requested

Where the DBH process asks for documentation or additional details, provide information that you are authorized to submit.

04

Wait for review

Submission does not automatically mean publication or verification. Review status and publication decisions are handled by DBH.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQs

Common questions about searching, accounts, enquiries, verification and support.

Do I need an account to browse properties?

No. Public marketplace pages such as the home page, Properties and Help & FAQs can be viewed without signing in. Account-only tools require authentication.

Do I need an account to save a property?

Yes. Saved properties are associated with the DBH account/browser session used by the site.

How do I contact DBH?

Use the public Contact Us page or open the Customer Care headset icon on the site.

How do I find a specific property?

Use the marketplace filters or search for its DBH property code. Opening the full listing gives you the most useful context.

What does “Verified Property” mean?

It indicates the verification state recorded for that listing by DBH. It is not a replacement for legal or professional due diligence.

What does “Verification pending” mean?

The listing has not reached the displayed verified state at that time.

Can I list land?

Yes. Use List a property and provide the relevant information requested by DBH.

Can I list commercial property?

Yes. Commercial property is one of the categories supported by the marketplace.

How do I reset my password?

Go to Forgot password, enter your account email and follow the recovery instructions.

Does DBH use Supabase for Google sign-in?

No. Google sign-in on DBH uses Google Identity Services and the DBH authentication server. Supabase remains part of the site's backend data infrastructure.

Will DBH always show a property as available?

Listing information can change. Confirm availability with DBH before making important decisions or arranging a transaction.

Are demo properties real properties?

Demo/test records are development records and should not be treated as proof of real-world ownership or legal title.

Can I use DBH on my phone?

Yes. The public website is responsive, and DBH also supports an installable PWA experience on compatible browsers.

How do I contact support about an account problem?

Open Customer Care or use the Contact Us page and include enough detail for DBH to identify the problem without sharing passwords or secret credentials.

Can I request a property viewing?

Yes. Use the enquiry form and select the viewing-related option where available.

Should I pay money because a listing is marked verified?

Verification status alone is not an instruction to pay. Confirm the transaction terms, documentation and appropriate independent checks before payment.

Need more help?

Open Customer Care from the floating headset icon for the fastest supported route, or send DBH a detailed message through the public contact page.
