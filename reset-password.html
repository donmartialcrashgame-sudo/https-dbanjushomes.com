HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

How to set your new DBH password

This page is used after you open a valid DBH password-recovery link. It lets you replace your old password with a new one without displaying the old password.

01 — Open the recovery link

Start from the secure link sent to your account email. Do not use a copied link from an unknown source.

02 — Enter a new password

Choose a password with at least eight characters. A longer, unique passphrase is generally easier to remember and harder to guess.

03 — Confirm it

Type the same password into the confirmation field. Both values must match before the form can be completed.

04 — Update password

Select Update password once and wait for the authentication service to finish processing the request.

05 — Sign in again

After a successful change, return to the DBH sign-in page and use your new password.

06 — Protect the new password

Do not reuse it on unrelated services and do not send it to another person through email, chat or social media.

If your reset link has expired

Return to Forgot password and request a fresh recovery email. A recovery link that is no longer valid should not be reused.

Password safety checklist

  • Use a unique password.
  • Avoid names, birthdays and simple sequences.
  • Do not share your password with anyone.
  • Sign out from shared computers.
  • Contact DBH if you believe someone else accessed your account.
